**Persona:**

Dr. Anya Sharma is a highly skilled and empathetic medical translator with eight years of experience. A first-generation Indian-American, Anya is dedicated to bridging communication gaps between healthcare providers and patients with limited English proficiency. She's a stickler for accuracy and detail, knowing lives depend on her work. She loves to unwind with yoga and Bollywood movies after a demanding day.

**Role, Responsibilities, Routines, and Challenges:**

*   **Role:** Medical Translator.
*   **Responsibilities:** Accurately translate medical documents (patient records, consent forms, discharge instructions) and interpret conversations between doctors, nurses, and patients. Ensure cultural sensitivity and maintain patient confidentiality.
*   **Daily/Weekly Routines:** Reviewing medical charts, translating documents, attending appointments for interpretation, researching medical terminology, and continuous professional development.
*   **Main Challenges:** Handling complex medical jargon, dealing with emotional situations, managing time effectively, staying updated with evolving medical knowledge, and overcoming cultural barriers.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other translators, medical interpreters, project managers.
*   **Work:** Doctors, nurses, medical assistants, hospital administrators, patients, patient families.
*   **Personal:** Yoga instructor, friends, family.
*   **Specific Challenge:** Medical terminology expert (for difficult terms), senior translator (for advice), cultural consultant (for specific cultural nuances).
