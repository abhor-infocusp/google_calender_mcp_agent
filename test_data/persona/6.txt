**Persona:** Maria is a 45-year-old school bus driver in a suburban town. She's been driving the same route for 10 years, enjoys the kids, and prides herself on her safety record. She is a single mother and lives with her teenage son.

**Role:** Safely transport students to and from school and school-related activities.

**Responsibilities & Routine:**

*   Pre-trip inspection of the bus.
*   Follow a strict route and schedule.
*   Maintain order on the bus.
*   Communicate with school staff and parents.
*   Daily cleaning and fueling of the bus.

**Challenges:**

*   Dealing with disruptive children.
*   Navigating traffic and adverse weather conditions.
*   Maintaining a strict schedule.
*   Vehicle maintenance issues.
*   Stress from responsibility for children's safety.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other bus drivers, dispatchers, mechanics.
*   **Work:** School principal, teachers, students, parents.
*   **Personal:** Son, neighbors.
*   **Specific Challenge (Discipline):** School counselor, parents of specific children.
