**Persona:**

Maria Rodriguez is a vibrant, community-focused school librarian at a large, diverse urban high school. A former English teacher, she transitioned to librarianship ten years ago, aiming to foster a love of reading and research skills among teenagers. She is married with two children, actively involved in her local community garden, and a passionate advocate for literacy programs.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Routines:**

*   **Role:** Facilitate access to information and resources, promote literacy, and support the school's curriculum.
*   **Responsibilities:** Collection development, managing library budget, assisting students and teachers with research, teaching information literacy skills, organizing reading programs and events, maintaining the library's physical and digital spaces.
*   **Daily/Weekly Routines:** Morning book club with students, research skills instruction for English classes, curating online resources for specific assignments, assisting individual students with research projects, ordering new books and materials, collaborating with teachers on curriculum development, and planning author visits.
*   **Main Challenges:** Limited budget, outdated resources, keeping students engaged with reading in the digital age, combating misinformation, advocating for the library's importance within the school, and managing diverse student needs.

*   **Team:** Library assistant, student volunteers.
