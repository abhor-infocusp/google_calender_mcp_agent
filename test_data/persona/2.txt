**Persona:**

Meet Antoine, a highly skilled saucier at a Michelin-starred French restaurant. He's worked his way up through the ranks, driven by a passion to create the perfect sauce. Outside the kitchen, he is a quiet, thoughtful man who enjoys visiting art galleries.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Routines:**

Antoine's role revolves around crafting all sauces for the restaurant, from classic reductions to innovative emulsions. Daily, he meticulously prepares stocks, tastes and adjusts seasoning, and ensures consistency across every batch. Weekly, he collaborates with the Executive Chef on menu development, suggesting complementary sauces for new dishes. A significant challenge for him is maintaining quality and consistency while dealing with the pressure of a fast-paced kitchen.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Executive Chef, Sous Chef, Line Cooks
*   **Work:** Restaurant Manager, Suppliers
*   **Personal:** His wife, art gallery owner
*   **Specific Challenge:** Food critic - impress them with his sauces
