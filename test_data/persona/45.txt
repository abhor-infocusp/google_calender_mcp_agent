**Persona:**

Aisha is a 32-year-old Product UX Designer at a mid-sized e-commerce company. She's passionate about creating intuitive and enjoyable user experiences that drive business goals. Outside of work, Aisha enjoys hiking, cooking, and volunteering at a local animal shelter.

**Role:**

Product UX Designer

**Responsibilities:**

*   Conduct user research and usability testing
*   Create wireframes, prototypes, and user flows
*   Collaborate with product managers and engineers to define product requirements
*   Design intuitive and engaging user interfaces
*   Iterate on designs based on user feedback and data analysis

**Daily/Weekly Routines:**

*   Daily stand-up meetings with the product team
*   User research sessions (2-3 times per week)
*   Design reviews and critiques
*   Prototyping and wireframing
*   Data analysis and reporting

**Main Challenges:**

*   Balancing user needs with business goals
*   Communicating design decisions effectively to stakeholders
*   Keeping up with the latest UX trends and technologies
*   Working with limited resources and tight deadlines

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Product Manager, UI Designer, Front-End Engineer, Back-End Engineer, QA Tester
*   **Work:** UX Research Lead, Marketing Team, Customer Support Team
*   **Personal:** Partner, Friends, Family
*   **Specific Challenge (Balancing User Needs & Business Goals):** Stakeholders from Sales and Marketing.
