**Persona:**

Maria is a 35-year-old rehabilitation personal trainer working in a private physical therapy clinic. She's passionate about helping people regain their strength and mobility after injuries or surgeries. A former college athlete who had to overcome a knee injury, she understands the physical and emotional challenges her clients face.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Routine:**

*   **Role:** Guide clients through exercise programs designed to restore function, reduce pain, and improve overall well-being.
*   **Responsibilities:** Assessing clients' physical condition, developing personalized rehabilitation plans, monitoring progress, and modifying exercises as needed.
*   **Daily Routine:** Reviewing client files, one-on-one training sessions (6-8 per day), collaborating with physical therapists, documenting client progress, and staying updated on rehabilitation techniques.
*   **Main Challenges:** Dealing with clients' pain and frustration, managing setbacks in recovery, motivating clients to adhere to their programs, and ensuring exercises are performed safely and effectively.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Physical therapists, occupational therapists, doctors, clinic manager, and other personal trainers.
*   **Work:** Clients with various injuries or conditions (e.g., post-operative patients, athletes recovering from sprains, individuals with chronic pain).
*   **Personal:** Husband, family, friends, and her own physical trainer (to maintain her physical health).
*   **Specific Challenge:** Doctors specializing in pain management or orthopedic surgeons (when clients require further medical intervention).
