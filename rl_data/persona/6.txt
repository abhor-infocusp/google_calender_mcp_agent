**Persona:**

Meet Sarah, a mid-career grant writer for a non-profit focused on environmental conservation. Passionate about nature, she juggles the demands of securing funding with a desire to see tangible change in her community. Sarah is meticulous and driven, yet often feels the pressure of tight deadlines and the uncertainty of grant approvals.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Routines:**

*   **Role:** Secure funding for the non-profit through grant applications.
*   **Responsibilities:** Research grant opportunities, write compelling proposals, manage application deadlines, and report on grant outcomes.
*   **Daily/Weekly Routines:** Spend mornings researching funding opportunities, afternoons writing and editing proposals, and Fridays dedicated to reporting and administrative tasks.
*   **Main Challenges:** Facing rejection, dealing with complex application guidelines, competing with larger organizations, and demonstrating impact with limited resources.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:**
    *   Executive Director: Approves grant strategies.
    *   Program Managers: Provide project details.
    *   Development Director: Coordinates fundraising efforts.
*   **Work:**
    *   Grant Officers (at Foundations): Clarify guidelines.
    *   Other Grant Writers: Share resources and advice.
*   **Personal:**
    *   Friends & Family: Emotional support.
*   **Specific Challenge:**
    *   Data Analyst: Helps demonstrate the impact of the non-profit's programs.
