Let's choose **Vertical Farmer**.

##################

**Persona:**

Elena Rodriguez, 35, is a driven vertical farmer. She left a corporate job to pursue sustainable agriculture, running a small indoor farm in Detroit, Michigan. She is passionate about providing fresh, local produce to her community.

**Role:**

Farm Owner/Operator

**Responsibilities:**

*   Managing hydroponic systems
*   Controlling environmental factors (light, temperature, humidity)
*   Planting, harvesting, and packaging crops
*   Marketing and sales to local restaurants and markets
*   Maintaining equipment and infrastructure
*   Managing finances and inventory

**Daily/Weekly Routine:**

*   Daily monitoring of plant health and system performance
*   Weekly harvesting and planting cycles
*   Regular cleaning and sanitation
*   Weekly deliveries to clients
*   Monthly inventory checks and financial reviews

**Main Challenges:**

*   High energy costs
*   Pest and disease control in a closed environment
*   Competition from traditional agriculture
*   Securing funding and investment

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Two part-time employees for harvesting and maintenance.
*   **Work:** Local chefs, grocery store managers, hydroponics equipment supplier, energy consultant.
*   **Personal:** Family (support system), friends from her previous job.
*   **Specific Challenge:** Agricultural extension agent (for pest control advice), other vertical farmers (for networking and best practices).
