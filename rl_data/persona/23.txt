**Persona:**

Maria is a 35-year-old oncology nurse working at a large urban hospital. She is passionate about providing compassionate care to her patients and their families during a difficult time. A dedicated professional, she balances her demanding career with raising two young children.

**Role, Responsibilities, Routines, and Challenges:**

*   **Role:** Provide specialized care and support to cancer patients.
*   **Responsibilities:** Administer chemotherapy, monitor patients' conditions, manage symptoms, educate patients and families, and offer emotional support.
*   **Daily Routine:** Start the day reviewing patient charts and preparing medications. She spends the majority of her time administering treatments, assessing patients, and addressing their concerns. She collaborates with doctors and other healthcare professionals.
*   **Main Challenges:** Dealing with the emotional toll of patient suffering and loss, managing heavy workloads, and staying updated on the latest advancements in cancer treatment.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Oncologists, pharmacists, other nurses, social workers, palliative care specialists.
*   **Work:** Hospital administrators, lab technicians, patient transport staff.
*   **Personal:** Husband, children, parents, close friends.
*   **Specific Challenges:** Support group for oncology nurses, therapist.
