**Persona:**

Sarah is a 32-year-old video game writer, currently working for a mid-sized studio known for its immersive RPGs. Outside of work, she's an avid gamer and enjoys hiking.

*   **Role:** Narrative Designer/Writer
*   **Responsibilities:** Crafting compelling storylines, writing character dialogue, developing lore, and ensuring narrative consistency within the game world.
*   **Daily/Weekly Routine:** She spends her mornings brainstorming with the design team, afternoons writing and revising scripts, and evenings playtesting builds to identify narrative issues. Weekly tasks include attending meetings to discuss story arcs and providing feedback on character design.
*   **Main Challenges:** Balancing creative vision with technical limitations, maintaining a cohesive narrative across a sprawling game world, and dealing with changing project requirements.
