**Persona:**

Meet Sarah, a 35-year-old tax accountant at a mid-sized firm. She's meticulous and dedicated, balancing work with raising her two young children. She loves helping clients navigate complex tax laws but struggles with the ever-changing regulations.

**Role:** Tax Accountant

**Responsibilities:**

*   Prepare and file tax returns for individuals and businesses.
*   Advise clients on tax planning strategies.
*   Research tax laws and regulations.
*   Represent clients in audits.

**Daily/Weekly Routine:**

*   Review client financial documents.
*   Prepare tax calculations and filings.
*   Communicate with clients regarding their tax situations.
*   Stay updated on tax law changes.

**Main Challenges:**

*   Keeping up with constantly changing tax laws.
*   Dealing with demanding clients.
*   Meeting tight deadlines during tax season.
*   Maintaining work-life balance.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other accountants, tax preparers, firm partners
*   **Work:** Clients, IRS agents
*   **Personal:** Spouse, children, family
