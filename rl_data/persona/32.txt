**Persona:**

Maria Rodriguez, 35, is the restaurant manager of "La Casita," a family-owned Mexican restaurant in a bustling city neighborhood. She's worked there since she was 16, starting as a busser and working her way up. Maria is dedicated to maintaining the restaurant's warm atmosphere and high food quality, which her customers have come to love.

**Role:** Restaurant Manager

**Responsibilities:**

*   Overseeing daily operations
*   Managing staff (hiring, training, scheduling)
*   Ensuring customer satisfaction
*   Inventory management and ordering
*   Maintaining quality control
*   Financial performance

**Typical Routine:**

*   Morning: Check inventory, review reservations, prepare staff schedule.
*   Afternoon: Supervise lunch service, handle customer complaints, address any maintenance issues.
*   Evening: Oversee dinner service, reconcile daily sales, address staff concerns, plan for the next day.

**Main Challenges:**

*   Balancing customer satisfaction with cost control.
*   Dealing with employee turnover and shortages.
*   Keeping up with food trends and competition.
*   Maintaining a positive work environment.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Servers, cooks, bartenders, bussers, dishwashers, hostess.
*   **Work:** Restaurant owner (her uncle), food suppliers, health inspectors, repair technicians, pest control.
*   **Personal:** Husband, two children, close friends from high school.
