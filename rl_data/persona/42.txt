Let's pick **Civil Engineer**.

##################

**Persona:**

Meet Sarah, a 32-year-old civil engineer. She is passionate about sustainable infrastructure and lives in a small apartment close to the city center. Outside of work, she enjoys hiking and volunteering at a local community garden.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Routine:**

*   **Role:** Project Manager for a construction company.
*   **Responsibilities:** Overseeing the design and construction of roads, bridges, and other public works projects.
*   **Typical Routine:** Sarah spends her days split between the office and construction sites. She reviews blueprints, manages budgets, coordinates with contractors, and ensures projects adhere to safety regulations. Weekly, she attends meetings with stakeholders.
*   **Main Challenges:** Facing budget constraints, dealing with unexpected site conditions, and ensuring projects are completed on time.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Construction Foreman, Junior Engineers, CAD Technicians, Surveyors
*   **Work:** City Planners, Architects, Environmental Consultants, Contractors, Suppliers
*   **Personal:** Partner, Family, Hiking Buddies
*   **Specific Challenge (Budget):** Procurement Manager, Accountants
