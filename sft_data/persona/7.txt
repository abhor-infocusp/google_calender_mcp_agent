**Persona:** Maria is a 52-year-old hospice nurse. She finds fulfillment in providing comfort and dignity to patients in their final stages of life. A widow herself, she understands loss deeply and connects with patients and families on a personal level.

**Role:** Hospice Nurse

**Responsibilities and Routine:**

*   Managing pain and symptoms.
*   Providing emotional and spiritual support.
*   Educating families on caregiving.
*   Coordinating with doctors and other healthcare professionals.
*   Visiting patients in their homes or hospice facilities (daily).
*   Documenting patient conditions and care plans (daily).
*   Attending team meetings (weekly).

**Challenges:**

*   Emotional toll of dealing with death and grief.
*   Balancing patient needs with family expectations.
*   Managing complex medication regimens.
*   Working with limited resources.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Doctors, social workers, chaplains, home health aides, other hospice nurses.
*   **Work:** Pharmacists, medical equipment suppliers.
*   **Personal:** Adult children, grief support group members, friends.
*   **Specific Challenge (Emotional Toll):** Therapist, spiritual advisor.
