**Persona:**

Meet Sarah, a 28-year-old Data Analyst working for a medium-sized e-commerce company. She's passionate about uncovering insights from data and helping the company make informed decisions. Outside of work, she enjoys hiking and exploring new restaurants.

**Role, Responsibilities, Routines, and Challenges:**

*   **Role:** Data Analyst
*   **Responsibilities:** Collect, clean, and analyze data from various sources (website, sales, marketing). She then creates reports and dashboards to visualize findings. In doing so, she identifies trends and provides actionable recommendations.
*   **Daily/Weekly Routines:** Sarah spends her days writing SQL queries, building dashboards in Tableau, presenting insights to stakeholders, and attending meetings to understand business needs. Each week, she dedicates time to explore new data analysis techniques and tools.
*   **Main Challenges:** Dealing with messy or incomplete data, communicating complex findings to non-technical audiences, and keeping up with the ever-evolving data landscape are constant hurdles.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:**
    *   Data Engineer: Collaborates on data infrastructure.
    *   Other Data Analysts: Share knowledge and best practices.
    *   Data Science Manager: Provides guidance and support.
*   **Work:**
    *   Marketing Manager: Uses data insights to improve campaigns.
    *   Sales Director: Relies on data to track performance.
    *   Product Manager: Needs data to inform product development.
*   **Personal:**
    *   Friends from university (similar backgrounds): Offer support and networking.
*   **Specific Challenge (Communication):**
    *   Presentation coach: Helps improve communication skills.
