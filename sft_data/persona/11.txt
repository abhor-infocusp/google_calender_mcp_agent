**Persona:**

Maria Rodriguez is a mid-career grant writer working for a non-profit organization focused on providing affordable housing in urban areas. A dedicated professional in her late 30s, she juggles the demands of her job with raising two young children.

**Role, Responsibilities, Routines & Challenges:**

*   **Role:** Securing funding for the organization through grant proposals.
*   **Responsibilities:** Researching grant opportunities, writing compelling proposals, managing deadlines, and reporting on grant outcomes.
*   **Daily/Weekly Routine:** Spend mornings researching funding opportunities and working on grant narratives. Afternoons focus on budget development, data analysis, and communication with program staff.
*   **Main Challenges:** Tight deadlines, increasing competition for limited funding, and the need to constantly adapt to changing funder priorities.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Program Directors, Finance Officer, Executive Director.
*   **Work:** Foundation Program Officers, Government Agency Representatives, other Grant Writers (for networking).
*   **Personal:** Spouse, Children, Family.
*   **Specific Challenge:** Accountant (for financial reporting expertise).
