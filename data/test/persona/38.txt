**Persona:** Maria Rodriguez is a 35-year-old urban farmer. She converted a vacant lot in a low-income neighborhood into a thriving community garden and small-scale farm. She is passionate about providing fresh, affordable produce to her neighbors and educating them about healthy eating.

**Role:** Urban Farmer/Community Garden Manager

**Responsibilities:**

*   Planning and planting crops suited for urban environments.
*   Managing soil health and composting systems.
*   Organizing volunteers and community workshops.
*   Selling produce at local markets and to residents.
*   Securing funding through grants and donations.

**Daily/Weekly Routine:**

*   Morning: Watering, weeding, and harvesting crops.
*   Afternoon: Community outreach, volunteer management, and workshop preparation.
*   Weekly: Market sales, grant writing, and networking with local organizations.

**Main Challenges:**

*   Limited space and resources.
*   Soil contamination and urban pests.
*   Securing consistent funding.
*   Balancing farm work with community engagement.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Volunteers, interns, community members
*   **Work:** Local restaurant owners, farmers market manager, city council members
*   **Personal:** Family, friends, fellow urban farming enthusiasts
*   **Specific Challenge:** Grant writers, soil scientists, pest control specialists
