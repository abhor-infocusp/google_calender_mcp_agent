**Persona:**

Meet Sarah, a mid-career Data Analyst working for a medium-sized e-commerce company. She is passionate about uncovering trends and insights from data to improve business strategies. Outside work, she enjoys hiking and volunteering at a local animal shelter.

**Role:** Data Analyst

**Responsibilities:**

*   Collect and clean data from various sources.
*   Analyze data using statistical techniques and tools.
*   Create reports and visualizations to communicate findings.
*   Collaborate with stakeholders to understand their data needs.
*   Identify opportunities for data-driven improvements.

**Daily/Weekly Routine:**

*   Daily: Check data pipelines, respond to data requests, attend team meetings.
*   Weekly: Deep-dive analysis projects, create presentations, meet with stakeholders.

**Main Challenges:**

*   Dealing with messy or incomplete data.
*   Communicating complex findings to non-technical audiences.
*   Keeping up with the latest data analysis tools and techniques.
*   Ensuring data privacy and security.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Data Engineers, other Data Analysts, Project Manager.
*   **Work:** Marketing Managers, Sales Directors, Product Owners.
*   **Personal:** Partner, Friends, Book Club members.
*   **Specific Challenge:** Data Science mentor (for keeping up with the latest tools).
