**Persona:**

Meet Priya, a 28-year-old product designer at a mid-sized e-commerce company. She lives in a trendy urban neighborhood and enjoys hiking and trying new restaurants. She is passionate about creating intuitive and delightful user experiences.

**Role:** Product Designer

**Responsibilities:**

*   Conduct user research and analyze data to identify user needs.
*   Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs.
*   Collaborate with engineers and product managers throughout the development process.
*   Conduct usability testing and iterate on designs based on feedback.
*   Maintain and evolve design systems.

**Typical Routine:**

*   Daily stand-up meetings with the product team.
*   Designing and prototyping new features.
*   Attending user research sessions.
*   Reviewing designs with stakeholders.
*   Working with developers to implement designs.

**Main Challenges:**

*   Balancing user needs with business goals.
*   Communicating design decisions effectively to non-designers.
*   Keeping up with the latest design trends and technologies.
*   Working with tight deadlines.
*   Ensuring consistency across all platforms.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Product Manager, Front-end Engineer, Back-end Engineer, UX Researcher, UI Designer, QA Tester.
*   **Work:** Stakeholders from Marketing and Sales, Customer Support representatives, Project Manager, other Product Designers.
*   **Personal:** Friends, family, hiking buddies.
*   **Specific Challenge:** Senior Product Designer (for mentorship), Design System Lead (for guidance on design system usage).
