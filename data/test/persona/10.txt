##################

**Persona:**

Maya is a mid-career technical recruiter at a rapidly growing software company. A driven professional, she balances her demanding job with an active lifestyle, enjoying hiking and yoga in her free time.

**Role:** Technical Recruiter

**Responsibilities:**

*   Sourcing qualified candidates for software engineering, data science, and IT positions.
*   Screening resumes and conducting initial phone interviews.
*   Coordinating technical interviews with hiring managers and team members.
*   Managing the candidate experience and providing feedback.
*   Negotiating job offers and onboarding new hires.

**Routines:**

*   Daily: Reviewing applications, conducting phone screens, attending team meetings.
*   Weekly: Meeting with hiring managers to discuss needs, attending industry events, updating the applicant tracking system.

**Challenges:**

*   Finding qualified candidates in a competitive market.
*   Keeping up with rapidly changing technologies.
*   Managing candidate expectations and ensuring a positive experience.
*   Balancing speed and quality in the hiring process.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other recruiters, HR generalists, recruiting coordinator.
*   **Work:** Hiring managers, engineering team leads, candidates.
*   **Personal:** Partner, friends from yoga class, hiking buddies.
*   **Specific Challenge (Finding Qualified Candidates):** LinkedIn contacts, industry conference attendees, university career services representatives.
