**Persona:**

Meet Sarah, a mid-career data analyst working for a medium-sized e-commerce company. She's passionate about uncovering trends and providing actionable insights that drive business decisions. Outside of work, Sarah enjoys hiking and is a volunteer data mentor for a local non-profit.

**Role, Responsibilities & Routines:**

*   **Role:** Data Analyst
*   **Responsibilities:** Collect, clean, and analyze large datasets related to customer behavior, sales, and marketing campaigns. Create dashboards and reports to visualize key performance indicators (KPIs). Communicate findings to stakeholders.
*   **Daily/Weekly Routines:** Daily data quality checks. Weekly meetings with marketing and sales teams to understand their data needs. Building reports using SQL and Python. Presenting insights to management.
*   **Main Challenges:** Data silos across different departments. Ensuring data accuracy and consistency. Communicating complex analytical findings to non-technical audiences. Keeping up with the latest data analysis tools and techniques.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other Data Analysts, Data Engineers, Data Scientists, Database Administrator, Analytics Manager.
*   **Work:** Marketing Managers, Sales Directors, Product Managers, IT Support.
*   **Personal:** Hiking friends, Volunteer Coordinator, Family.
*   **Specific Challenge (Data Silos):** Head of IT, Department Heads (Marketing, Sales, Product).
