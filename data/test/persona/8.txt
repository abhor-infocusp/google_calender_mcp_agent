**Persona:**

Mark is a 45-year-old carpenter who dedicates his free time to the local volunteer fire department in his small rural town. He's married with two teenage children and deeply committed to his community.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Challenges:**

*   **Role:** Volunteer Firefighter
*   **Responsibilities:** Responding to fire and rescue calls, providing emergency medical assistance, maintaining equipment, participating in training exercises, and community outreach.
*   **Routine:** Mark juggles his carpentry business with on-call duties. He attends weekly training sessions and responds to emergencies whenever dispatched, often at odd hours. Equipment checks and maintenance are ongoing tasks.
*   **Challenges:** Balancing work, family, and volunteer commitments; dealing with limited resources and funding; facing traumatic situations; and maintaining physical fitness.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other volunteer firefighters, fire chief, paramedics
*   **Work:** Clients, suppliers, construction workers.
*   **Personal:** Wife, children, neighbors, friends
*   **Specific Challenge:** Firefighting equipment suppliers, therapists or counselors (for critical incident stress).
