**Persona:**

*   **Role:** Medical Oncologist specializing in breast cancer.
*   **Responsibilities:** Diagnosing and treating cancer patients, developing treatment plans (chemotherapy, immunotherapy, targeted therapy), monitoring patient progress, providing supportive care, and participating in clinical trials.

*   **Main Challenges:** Dealing with treatment side effects, managing patient expectations, keeping up with the latest advancements in oncology, and maintaining emotional resilience in the face of loss.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Oncology nurses, radiation oncologists, surgeons, pathologists, pharmacists, research coordinators.
