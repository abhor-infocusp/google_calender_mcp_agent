**Persona:** Dr. Anya Sharma is a 42-year-old cardiothoracic surgeon. A dedicated professional, she balances a demanding career with raising two teenagers. She's known for her calm demeanor under pressure and her commitment to patient care, sometimes to the detriment of her personal life.

**Role:** Cardiothoracic Surgeon

**Responsibilities:** Performing heart and lung surgeries, diagnosing cardiovascular and thoracic conditions, developing treatment plans, leading a surgical team.

**Daily Routine:** Pre-operative patient consultations, performing surgeries (ranging from bypasses to transplants), post-operative care and rounds, attending meetings, reviewing patient data.

**Main Challenges:** Long and unpredictable hours, high-stress environment, emotional toll of dealing with critical illnesses, keeping up with medical advancements.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Surgical nurses, anesthesiologists, surgical technicians, perfusionists, other surgeons (Dr. Chen - senior mentor)
