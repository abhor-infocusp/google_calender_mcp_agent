**Persona:**

Meet Sarah, a 45-year-old hospice nurse. She's a compassionate caregiver, finding purpose in providing comfort and dignity to patients during their final stages of life. Balancing emotional resilience with personal well-being is a constant effort.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Routines:**

*   **Role:** Provide specialized care and support to terminally ill patients and their families.
*   **Responsibilities:** Pain management, symptom control, emotional support, educating families, coordinating care with physicians and other healthcare professionals.
*   **Daily/Weekly Routine:** Home visits to patients, assessing their condition, administering medication, providing emotional counseling, documenting care, attending team meetings.
*   **Main Challenges:** Witnessing patient decline and death, managing emotional stress, dealing with complex family dynamics, advocating for patient needs, and ensuring quality of life in limited time.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Physicians, social workers, chaplains, certified nursing assistants (CNAs), volunteers.
*   **Work:** Pharmacists (medication management), medical equipment suppliers, hospice administrators.
*   **Personal:** Husband, children, close friends, therapist.
*   **Specific Challenge (Burnout):** Support group members, mindfulness instructor.
