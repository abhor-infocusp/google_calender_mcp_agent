**Persona:**

Marco is a 35-year-old, Italian-born cruise ship chef. He's been working at sea for 10 years, supporting his family back home and seeing the world. He loves the energy of the kitchen but misses the stability of land-based life.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Routines:**

*   **Role:** Head Chef of a specialty Italian restaurant onboard a large cruise ship.
*   **Responsibilities:** Menu planning, ordering supplies, managing a team of cooks, ensuring food quality and safety, and maintaining guest satisfaction.
*   **Daily Routine:** Long hours (12-14 hours/day). Starts with inventory checks, followed by food preparation for lunch and dinner services. Supervises the kitchen team during peak hours, handles any issues, and ensures cleanliness. Rotates between different tasks throughout the week.
*   **Main Challenges:** Limited access to fresh ingredients, tight budgets, demanding passengers, crew dynamics, and extended periods away from family.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Sous Chefs, Line Cooks, Dishwashers, and Kitchen Staff.
*   **Work:** Food and Beverage Manager, Cruise Director, Purchasing Department, Restaurant Manager, and Sanitation Officers.
*   **Personal:** Wife and children back in Italy, close friends from culinary school.
*   **Specific Challenges:** Onboard therapist (to deal with stress and isolation), food suppliers (when specific ingredients are needed).
