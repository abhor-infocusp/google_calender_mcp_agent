**Persona:**

Meet Maria, a 42-year-old Commercial Plumber. She's been working in the field for 15 years and takes pride in her problem-solving skills. Outside of work, she enjoys hiking.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Challenges:**

*   **Role:** Commercial Plumber
*   **Responsibilities:** Installing and repairing plumbing systems in commercial buildings. Includes working with large-scale piping, complex drainage systems, and ensuring compliance with building codes.
*   **Daily/Weekly Routine:** Reading blueprints, installing new fixtures (sinks, toilets, water heaters), troubleshooting plumbing issues, performing maintenance checks, and coordinating with other construction workers.
*   **Main Challenges:** Dealing with outdated infrastructure, adhering to strict deadlines, working in cramped or hazardous conditions, and keeping up with evolving plumbing technologies.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Apprentice plumbers, other plumbers, construction foreman.
*   **Work:** Building managers, contractors, city inspectors, suppliers.
*   **Personal:** Husband, hiking group members.
