**Persona:** Maria is a 35-year-old HVAC technician, a single mom raising two kids. She's worked in the field for 10 years and takes pride in her ability to fix anything related to heating and cooling.

**Role:** HVAC Technician

**Responsibilities:** Installing, maintaining, and repairing heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems in residential and commercial buildings.

**Daily/Weekly Routine:**
*   Responding to service calls for HVAC malfunctions.
*   Performing routine maintenance on HVAC systems.
*   Installing new HVAC systems.
*   Diagnosing and repairing complex HVAC issues.
*   Ordering necessary parts and supplies.

**Main Challenges:**
*   Dealing with demanding customers.
*   Working in uncomfortable environments (hot attics, cold basements).
*   Keeping up with the latest HVAC technology.
*   Balancing work and family life.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Fellow HVAC technicians, dispatchers, supervisors.
*   **Work:** Homeowners, building managers, contractors, suppliers.
*   **Personal:** Children, family, friends.
*   **Specific Challenge (Work/Life Balance):** Support groups for single parents, daycare providers.
