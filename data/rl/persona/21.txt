**Persona:**

Marcus is a 35-year-old prison guard at a medium-security facility. A former high school athlete, he sought a stable job with benefits after his college plans fell through. He is married with two young children and values providing for his family above all else.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Routine:**

*   **Role:** Maintaining order and security within the prison.
*   **Responsibilities:** Supervising inmates, conducting searches, enforcing rules, responding to emergencies, writing reports.
*   **Daily Routine:** Begins with briefing, followed by rounds, monitoring inmate activities, supervising meals and recreation, and managing conflicts. Paperwork and incident reports fill the gaps.
*   **Main Challenges:** Understaffing, inmate aggression, maintaining impartiality, stress and burnout, and the emotional toll of the environment.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other prison guards, supervisors, medical staff, counselors.
*   **Work:** Inmates, Warden, visiting family members.
*   **Personal:** Wife, children, close friends from his hometown.
*   **Specific Challenge (Stress):** Therapist, support groups for correctional officers.
