**Persona:**

Meet Maria, a 35-year-old HVAC technician. A single mom, she's been in the field for 10 years and takes pride in providing comfort to families and businesses. She's currently working for a local HVAC company.

**Role, Responsibilities, Routines, and Challenges:**

*   **Role:** HVAC Technician
*   **Responsibilities:** Installation, maintenance, and repair of heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems. Diagnose and troubleshoot issues. Perform routine maintenance. Ensure systems meet safety standards.
*   **Daily/Weekly Routines:** Travel to job sites. Inspect equipment, run diagnostics, and conduct repairs. Install new systems. Order parts. Complete paperwork. Respond to emergency calls.
*   **Main Challenges:** Diagnosing complex system failures. Working in uncomfortable environments (hot attics, cramped spaces). Keeping up with rapidly changing technology. Long hours, especially during peak seasons. Dealing with frustrated customers.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other HVAC technicians, dispatchers, supervisors.
*   **Work:** Customers, suppliers, building managers.
*   **Personal:** Her daughter, family, friends.
*   **Specific Challenge:** Mentors with expertise in new HVAC technologies, trainers.
