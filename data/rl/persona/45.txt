Dr. Anya Sharma is a dedicated psychiatrist in a bustling urban mental health clinic. She balances a demanding caseload with a commitment to providing compassionate care, while also trying to maintain a healthy work-life balance for herself and her young family.

**Role:** Psychiatrist

**Responsibilities:**

*   Diagnosing and treating mental health disorders.
*   Providing therapy and counseling.
*   Prescribing and managing medication.
*   Conducting patient evaluations and assessments.
*   Collaborating with other healthcare professionals.

**Typical Weekly Routine:**

*   Monday-Friday: Patient appointments (therapy, medication management).
*   One evening per week: Group therapy session.
*   Weekly team meeting with social workers and psychologists.
*   Occasional weekend on-call duties.

**Main Challenges:**

*   High patient volume and limited appointment times.
*   Dealing with complex and severe mental illnesses.
*   Navigating insurance and bureaucratic hurdles.
*   Combating stigma surrounding mental health.
*   Preventing burnout.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Social workers, psychologists, nurses, administrative staff.
*   **Work:** Other psychiatrists, primary care physicians, hospital staff.
*   **Personal:** Spouse, children, friends, therapist.
*   **Specific Challenge (Burnout):** Supervisor, mentor, support group.
