**Persona:**

Meet Sarah, a 35-year-old Project Manager at a mid-sized software development company. She's meticulous, organized, and driven. A mother of two young children, she juggles work and family life with impressive efficiency, though she occasionally struggles to disconnect.

**Role:**

Sarah leads cross-functional teams to deliver software projects on time and within budget.

**Responsibilities & Routine:**

*   **Daily:** Stand-up meetings, task assignments, progress tracking, risk assessment, stakeholder communication.
*   **Weekly:** Project planning, sprint reviews, resource allocation, performance reports.

**Challenges:**

*   Scope creep
*   Conflicting priorities
*   Keeping team members motivated
*   Managing expectations from stakeholders

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Developers, testers, business analysts, UX designers.
*   **Work:** CEO, other project managers, department heads.
*   **Personal:** Spouse, children, parents, friends.
*   **Specific Challenge (Work-Life Balance):** Daycare provider, supportive colleagues.
