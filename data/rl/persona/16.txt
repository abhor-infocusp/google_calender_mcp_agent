**Persona:**

Meet Ricardo, a 45-year-old industrial electrician working at a large manufacturing plant. He's been in the trade for 20 years, providing for his wife and two teenage children. Ricardo takes pride in his precision and problem-solving skills, and he is a reliable presence at the plant.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Challenges:**

*   **Role:** Industrial Electrician
*   **Responsibilities:** Installing, maintaining, and repairing electrical systems and equipment in a manufacturing plant. This includes motors, generators, control systems, and complex machinery. Reads and interprets electrical schematics and ensures compliance with safety regulations.
*   **Daily/Weekly Routine:** Starts with a safety briefing, followed by assigned maintenance tasks or troubleshooting equipment failures. He spends time inspecting electrical systems, replacing faulty components, and conducting preventative maintenance. Some weeks involve project work, such as installing new equipment or upgrading existing systems.
*   **Main Challenges:** Diagnosing complex electrical issues under pressure to minimize downtime, keeping up with rapidly evolving technology, and adhering to strict safety protocols in a hazardous environment.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other electricians, maintenance technicians, mechanical engineers, plant supervisors
*   **Work:** Equipment vendors, safety inspectors, contractors
*   **Personal:** Wife, children, close friends from his apprenticeship days
*   **Specific Challenge:** Electrical engineer specializing in automation systems (for keeping up with rapidly evolving technology)
