**Persona:**

Maria is a 45-year-old single mother who has been driving a city bus for 15 years. She lives in a small apartment on the outskirts of town and works hard to provide for her teenage son.

**Role:** City Bus Driver

**Responsibilities:**

*   Safely operate a public transit bus according to a fixed schedule.
*   Collect fares and issue transfers.
*   Assist passengers with disabilities.
*   Maintain a clean and orderly bus.
*   Report any accidents or mechanical issues.

**Typical Routine:**

*   **Daily:** Pre-trip vehicle inspection, follow assigned route, manage passenger interactions, adhere to schedule, post-trip vehicle check.
*   **Weekly:** Attend safety meetings, complete paperwork, vehicle cleaning.

**Main Challenges:**

*   Dealing with difficult or unruly passengers.
*   Navigating heavy traffic and road closures.
*   Maintaining a strict schedule.
*   Long hours and irregular shifts.
*   Dealing with vehicle maintenance issues.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other bus drivers, dispatchers, mechanics, supervisors.
*   **Work:** Passengers, traffic controllers, transit authority staff.
*   **Personal:** Son, family members, neighbors.
*   **Specific Challenge:** Union representative (for workplace issues), social worker (financial or family support).
