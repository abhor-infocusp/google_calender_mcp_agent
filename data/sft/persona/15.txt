**Persona:** Maria Rodriguez is a 35-year-old HVAC technician, a single mother of two energetic boys. She's worked in the field for 10 years and recently started her own small HVAC repair and installation business.

**Role:** HVAC Technician/Business Owner

**Responsibilities:**

*   Diagnose and repair HVAC systems.
*   Install new heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems.
*   Perform routine maintenance on existing systems.
*   Order parts and manage inventory.
*   Schedule appointments and manage customer relations.
*   Handle billing and invoicing.

**Daily/Weekly Routine:**

*   Morning: Review schedule, prepare for appointments, and load equipment.
*   Daytime: Travel to job sites, diagnose problems, perform repairs/installations.
*   Evening: Complete paperwork, order parts, respond to customer inquiries, and manage business finances.
*   Weekends: Emergency calls, catching up on administrative tasks, and family time.

**Main Challenges:**

*   Balancing work and family responsibilities.
*   Competition from larger HVAC companies.
*   Finding and retaining qualified employees.
*   Dealing with demanding or unhappy customers.
*   Keeping up with the latest technology and industry standards.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** (If she had any) Junior technicians, apprentices (future)
*   **Work:** Suppliers (parts), other contractors (plumbers, electricians), building managers, homeowners.
*   **Personal:** Her children, her mother (babysitter), friends from trade school.
*   **Specific Challenge:** Business mentor (help with managing the business), accountant.
