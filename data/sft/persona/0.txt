**Persona:**

Meet Sarah, a 35-year-old Tax Accountant at a mid-sized firm in Chicago. She's driven and detail-oriented, balancing a demanding career with raising two young children. Her days are filled with tax codes, client meetings, and school pick-ups.

**Role, Responsibilities, Routines & Challenges:**

*   **Role:** Tax Accountant
*   **Responsibilities:** Preparing and filing tax returns for individuals and businesses, advising clients on tax planning strategies, ensuring compliance with tax laws, and representing clients in front of tax authorities.
*   **Daily/Weekly Routines:** Reviewing financial documents, preparing tax returns, meeting with clients, researching tax law changes, and responding to IRS inquiries. During tax season, long hours are standard.
*   **Main Challenges:** Keeping up with constantly evolving tax laws, managing client expectations, dealing with stressful deadlines, and maintaining work-life balance.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other tax accountants, tax partners, administrative staff.
*   **Work:** Clients (individuals, small business owners), IRS agents, financial advisors, lawyers.
*   **Personal:** Spouse, children, family, friends.
*   **Specific Challenge (Work-Life Balance):** Daycare providers, babysitters, other working parents.
