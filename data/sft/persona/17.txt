**Persona:**

Ms. Evans is a dedicated special education teacher in a public elementary school. She is in her early 30s and passionate about creating an inclusive and supportive learning environment for students with diverse needs. Outside of school, she enjoys hiking and spending time with her family.

**Role:** Special Education Teacher

**Responsibilities:**

*   Develop and implement individualized education programs (IEPs).
*   Adapt curriculum and teaching methods to meet diverse learning needs.
*   Assess student progress and adjust instructional strategies.
*   Collaborate with parents, general education teachers, and specialists.
*   Manage classroom behavior and promote social-emotional development.

**Typical Routines:**

*   Morning: Review student IEPs, prepare materials, and set up the classroom.
*   Daily: Provide individual and small-group instruction, implement behavior management strategies, and monitor student progress.
*   Weekly: Attend IEP meetings, communicate with parents, and collaborate with general education teachers.

**Main Challenges:**

*   Limited resources and large caseloads.
*   Balancing diverse student needs.
*   Managing challenging behaviors.
*   Navigating complex paperwork and compliance requirements.
*   Combating stigma and promoting inclusion.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** General education teachers, school psychologist, speech therapist, occupational therapist, paraprofessionals, school principal.
*   **Work:** Special education director, other special education teachers in the district.
*   **Personal:** Family, friends, support group for special education teachers.
*   **Specific Challenge:** Parents of students with disabilities, advocates for special education.
