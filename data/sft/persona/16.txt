**Persona:**

Meet Anya, a dedicated pastry chef at a high-end boutique hotel. With a passion for creating exquisite desserts, she balances artistry and precision, ensuring every pastry is a masterpiece. She is a perfectionist that can come off as a control freak. Outside the kitchen, she enjoys hiking and visiting art galleries.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Routines:**

*   **Role:** Pastry Chef
*   **Responsibilities:** Designs and prepares desserts, pastries, and baked goods for the hotel's restaurant, events, and afternoon tea service. Manage inventory, ensure quality control, and train junior pastry staff.
*   **Daily Routine:** Arrives early to prep doughs and ingredients. Oversees the pastry team, creates daily specials, decorates desserts, and manages orders.
*   **Main Challenges:** Maintaining consistent quality under pressure, managing staff, and keeping up with new culinary trends, especially with dietary restrictions.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Sous Chef, Junior Pastry Chefs, Kitchen Staff
*   **Work:** Hotel Manager, Food Suppliers, Restaurant Manager, Executive Chef
*   **Personal:** Hiking buddies, Art gallery friends
*   **Specific Challenge (Dietary Restrictions):** Hotel guests with allergies, Dieticians
