**Persona:**

Meet Maria, a 35-year-old HVAC technician. She's been in the field for 10 years, starting right after completing her vocational training. Maria is a single mother raising her 8-year-old son, Liam. She takes pride in her ability to troubleshoot complex systems and enjoys the satisfaction of restoring comfort to homes and businesses.

**Role:** HVAC Technician

**Responsibilities:**

*   Installing, maintaining, and repairing heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems.
*   Diagnosing malfunctions and troubleshooting HVAC equipment.
*   Performing preventative maintenance.
*   Adhering to safety protocols and regulations.

**Routines:**

*   Daily: Responding to service calls, diagnosing problems, making repairs, and completing paperwork.
*   Weekly: Stocking service van, attending team meetings, and ordering parts.

**Challenges:**

*   Dealing with demanding clients and tight deadlines.
*   Keeping up with rapidly changing technology.
*   Working in uncomfortable environments (extreme heat/cold, cramped spaces).

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Lead Technician (supervisor), fellow HVAC Technicians, dispatchers.
*   **Work:** Clients (homeowners, business managers), suppliers, building inspectors.
*   **Personal:** Liam (son), her mother (babysitter), neighbors.
*   **Specific Challenge:** Technical trainers (for keeping up with new tech), financial advisor (managing single-parent finances).
