**Persona:**

Mr. Harrison is a dedicated high school history teacher in a rural town. He is passionate about making history relevant to his students' lives, despite limited resources. He coaches the debate team and advises the student government. Outside of school, he enjoys hiking and spending time with his family.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Routines:**

*   **Role:** High School History Teacher
*   **Responsibilities:** Lesson planning, delivering lectures, grading assignments, preparing students for standardized tests, sponsoring extracurricular activities.
*   **Daily Routine:** Arrive early to prepare, teach four classes, grade papers during lunch, meet with students after school, coach debate team twice a week, attend faculty meetings.
*   **Main Challenges:** Student apathy, lack of funding for resources, standardized testing pressures, balancing work and personal life.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other history teachers, school principal, guidance counselors.
*   **Work:** Students, parents, librarian, special education staff.
*   **Personal:** Wife, children, hiking buddies.
*   **Specific Challenge (funding):** School board members, local business owners, grant writers.
