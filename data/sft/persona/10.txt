Let's go with **Computer Technician**.

##################

**Persona:**

Meet Alex, a mid-30s computer technician working for a small IT support company in a rural town. Alex enjoys the problem-solving aspect of the job and the satisfaction of helping people who are less tech-savvy. Outside work, Alex is a keen gamer and enjoys hiking in the nearby hills.

**Role:**

Computer Technician

**Responsibilities/Routine:**

*   Diagnosing and repairing hardware/software issues for clients (businesses and individuals).
*   Installing and configuring computer systems and networks.
*   Providing technical support via phone, email, or in-person.
*   Performing routine maintenance and upgrades.
*   Weekly tasks include inventory management and documentation.
*   Stays updated with the latest technology trends.

**Main Challenges:**

*   Dealing with frustrated clients who lack technical understanding.
*   Keeping up with the ever-changing landscape of computer technology.
*   Troubleshooting complex issues with limited resources.
*   Long hours on-site fixing urgent issues.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other technicians, dispatch manager.
*   **Work:** Clients (small business owners, individuals), IT vendors.
*   **Personal:** Partner, hiking friends, online gaming community.
*   **Specific Challenge (Staying Updated):** Online course instructors, tech bloggers, industry forum members.
