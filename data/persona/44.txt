##################

**Persona:**

Marco is a 45-year-old Head Chef at a bustling Italian restaurant in downtown Chicago. A divorced father of two, he dedicates his life to his craft, finding solace and purpose in the controlled chaos of the kitchen. Years of experience etched on his face, he demands perfection.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Routines:**

Marco is responsible for the entire kitchen operation. His duties include menu planning, ordering supplies, managing inventory, overseeing food preparation, ensuring quality control, and maintaining kitchen hygiene. Each day begins with inspecting ingredients. He leads the dinner service. Marco handles customer complaints, and trains junior staff. He works six days a week, often late into the night.

**Challenges:**

Staff turnover, maintaining consistency, dealing with demanding customers, managing food costs, and staying innovative with the menu while honoring tradition.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Sous Chef, Line Cooks, Dishwashers, Servers, Kitchen Manager
*   **Work:** Restaurant Owner, Food Suppliers, Health Inspectors
*   **Personal:** His children, Ex-wife
*   **Specific Challenge:** Accountant (for managing food costs), Restaurant Consultant (for menu innovation)
