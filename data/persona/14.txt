**Persona:**

Meet Frank, a 45-year-old Industrial Electrician at a large manufacturing plant. With over 20 years in the trade, he's seen it all. He's a family man, enjoys fishing on weekends, and takes pride in keeping the plant running smoothly.

**Role:** Industrial Electrician

**Responsibilities:**

*   Install, maintain, and repair electrical systems in an industrial setting.
*   Troubleshoot electrical equipment malfunctions.
*   Conduct routine inspections to prevent breakdowns.
*   Read and interpret electrical blueprints and schematics.
*   Ensure compliance with safety regulations.

**Daily/Weekly Routine:**

Frank starts his day reviewing work orders. Mornings are often spent on preventative maintenance – checking motors, control panels, and wiring. Afternoons are typically dedicated to addressing breakdowns or new installations. Weekly, he participates in safety meetings and assists with larger projects.

**Main Challenges:**

*   Working with high-voltage equipment.
*   Diagnosing complex electrical problems quickly.
*   Keeping up with rapidly changing technology.
*   Meeting tight deadlines during plant shutdowns.
*   Ensuring safety protocols are followed meticulously.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other electricians, maintenance technicians, plant engineers.
*   **Work:** Plant manager, machine operators, safety officer, electrical suppliers.
*   **Personal:** Wife, kids, fishing buddies.
*   **Specific Challenge:** Vendor representatives for new equipment training, electrical inspector.
