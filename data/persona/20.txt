I have randomly selected "Pharmacy Clerk".

##################

**Persona:** Maria Rodriguez is a 42-year-old pharmacy clerk at a busy local drugstore. A single mother, she works hard to provide for her teenage son while balancing the demands of her job.

**Role:** Pharmacy clerks assist pharmacists with dispensing medications and managing the pharmacy's operations.

**Responsibilities & Routine:**

*   Greeting customers and taking prescriptions.
*   Collecting patient information and updating records.
*   Counting medications and preparing labels.
*   Handling payments and insurance claims.
*   Managing inventory and stocking shelves.
*   Answering phone calls and addressing customer inquiries.

**Challenges:**

*   Dealing with demanding or impatient customers.
*   Accuracy when handling prescriptions.
*   Staying up-to-date with new medications and regulations.
*   Standing for long periods.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Pharmacist (supervisor), other pharmacy clerks, pharmacy technicians.
*   **Work:** Doctors (prescribers), insurance company representatives, drug suppliers.
*   **Personal:** Son, family members, friends.
*   **Specific Challenge:** Regular Customers
