**Persona:**

Meet Frank, a 45-year-old industrial electrician working at a large manufacturing plant. He's been in the trade for over 20 years. Frank is a reliable, no-nonsense guy who takes pride in keeping the plant running smoothly. He's a family man with two kids in college and enjoys fishing on the weekends.

**Role:** Industrial Electrician

**Responsibilities:**

*   Installing and maintaining electrical systems in a manufacturing plant.
*   Troubleshooting electrical issues with machinery and equipment.
*   Performing preventative maintenance on electrical systems.
*   Ensuring compliance with safety regulations.
*   Reading and interpreting electrical schematics.

**Typical Routines:**

*   Daily equipment checks and maintenance.
*   Responding to equipment breakdowns and emergencies.
*   Working on planned electrical upgrades and installations.
*   Documenting all work performed.

**Main Challenges:**

*   Dealing with aging electrical infrastructure.
*   Keeping up with new technologies and automation.
*   Working in hazardous environments.
*   Minimizing downtime during repairs.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other electricians, maintenance mechanics, plant engineers.
*   **Work:** Plant manager, production supervisors, equipment vendors.
*   **Personal:** Wife, kids, fishing buddies.
*   **Specific Challenge (Aging Infrastructure):** Electrical engineers specializing in retrofitting old systems, vendors of modern electrical equipment.
