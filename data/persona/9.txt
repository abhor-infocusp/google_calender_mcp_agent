**Persona:**

Meet Sarah, a mid-career Data Analyst working for a medium-sized e-commerce company. She is methodical and enjoys uncovering hidden trends in large datasets. Outside of work, she enjoys hiking and is a passionate amateur photographer.

**Role:**

Data Analysts transform raw data into actionable insights that inform business decisions.

**Responsibilities:**

*   Collect and clean data from various sources.
*   Analyze data using statistical techniques and tools (SQL, Python, R).
*   Develop dashboards and visualizations to communicate findings.
*   Present insights and recommendations to stakeholders.

**Routines:**

*   Daily: Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and address anomalies. Respond to ad-hoc data requests.
*   Weekly: Prepare reports on sales trends, customer behavior, or marketing campaign performance. Attend meetings to discuss findings and propose data-driven solutions.

**Challenges:**

*   Ensuring data quality and accuracy.
*   Communicating complex data insights to non-technical audiences.
*   Keeping up with the latest data analysis tools and techniques.
*   Dealing with large and complex datasets.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other Data Analysts, Data Engineers, Database Administrators.
*   **Work:** Marketing Managers, Sales Directors, Product Managers.
*   **Personal:** Hiking buddies, photography club members.
*   **Specific Challenge (Data Quality):** Data Governance Manager.
