**Persona:**

Meet Sarah, a mid-career data analyst working for a medium-sized e-commerce company. She's detail-oriented, enjoys problem-solving, and is passionate about uncovering insights from data. Outside of work, Sarah is an avid hiker and volunteers at a local animal shelter.

**Role, Responsibilities, Routines, and Challenges:**

*   **Role:** Data Analyst
*   **Responsibilities:** Collect, clean, and analyze data related to customer behavior, sales trends, and marketing campaign performance. Create reports and dashboards to visualize findings. Present insights and recommendations to stakeholders.
*   **Daily/Weekly Routine:** Pulling data from various databases, writing SQL queries, using Python and data analysis libraries for analysis, building visualizations with tools like Tableau, attending meetings with marketing and sales teams, and preparing presentations.
*   **Main Challenges:** Dealing with messy or incomplete data, communicating complex findings to non-technical audiences, keeping up with the latest data analysis techniques, and ensuring data privacy and security.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other Data Analysts, Data Engineers, Database Administrators, Project Manager.
*   **Work:** Marketing Managers, Sales Directors, Customer Service Representatives, IT Support.
*   **Personal:** Spouse/Partner, Friends, Hiking Group Members.
*   **Specific Challenge (Data Quality):** Data Governance Officer, IT Security Specialist.
