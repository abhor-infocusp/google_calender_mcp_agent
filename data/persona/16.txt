**Persona:**

Meet Antoine, a meticulous and passionate Saucier at a high-end French restaurant. Nearing 40, he's dedicated his life to mastering sauces, stocks, and reductions. Divorced and childless, his world revolves around the kitchen.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Routines:**

Antoine is responsible for all sauces, stocks, and gravies served. Daily, he begins by checking inventory and prepping ingredients. During service, he flawlessly executes sauces to order, ensuring perfect consistency and flavor. Evenings involve cleaning his station and preparing stocks for the next day. A weekly task includes creating new sauce specials, experimenting with flavors.

**Main Challenges:**

Maintaining consistency under pressure is tough. Also, sourcing high-quality ingredients and dealing with the restaurant's demanding head chef present difficulties.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Sous Chef (direct supervisor), Line Cooks (collaborators), Dishwashers (support).
*   **Work:** Executive Chef (authority), Restaurant Manager (resource allocation), Suppliers (ingredients).
*   **Personal:** Former spouse (occasional contact), fellow chefs (camaraderie).
*   **Specific Challenge:** Head Chef (pressure, criticism).
