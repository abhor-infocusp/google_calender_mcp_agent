**Persona:**

Maria is a 32-year-old ICU nurse at a large city hospital. She's dedicated, empathetic, and highly skilled. Outside work, she enjoys hiking and spending time with her dog, but the demanding nature of her job often leaves her exhausted.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Routine:**

*   **Role:** ICU Nurse
*   **Responsibilities:** Monitor vital signs, administer medications, manage ventilators, provide wound care, respond to emergencies, and support patients' families.
*   **Typical Routine:** 12-hour shifts involving constant patient monitoring, charting, collaborating with doctors, and performing life-saving interventions.
*   **Main Challenges:** High patient acuity, emotional toll of dealing with critical illness and death, long hours, and potential for burnout.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Doctors, respiratory therapists, other nurses, pharmacists, nurse practitioners, dietitians.
*   **Work:** Hospital administrators, lab technicians, social workers, chaplains.
*   **Personal:** Partner, family, friends, hiking buddies.
*   **Specific Challenges (Burnout):** Therapist, support group members, mentor.
