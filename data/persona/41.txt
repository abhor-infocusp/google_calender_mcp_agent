**Persona:** Maria Rodriguez is a 25-year-old, recently single mother working as a hotel clerk at a mid-range hotel near the city center. She is working towards a promotion to front desk supervisor to provide a better life for her 4-year-old daughter.

**Role:** Hotel Clerk

**Responsibilities:**

*   Check guests in/out efficiently and courteously.
*   Handle reservations and cancellations.
*   Answer phone calls and address guest inquiries.
*   Manage guest accounts and process payments.
*   Resolve guest complaints and escalate issues when necessary.
*   Maintain a clean and organized front desk area.

**Daily Routine:**

*   Arrive early to review the logbook from the previous shift.
*   Greet guests, process check-ins/check-outs.
*   Answer phone inquiries, make reservations.
*   Handle guest requests (wake-up calls, directions).
*   Reconcile daily transactions before shift end.

**Main Challenges:**

*   Dealing with rude or demanding guests.
*   Handling overbookings and finding alternative accommodations.
*   Staying calm and efficient during peak hours.
*   Balancing work with childcare responsibilities.
*   Low wages and limited opportunities for advancement.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Front Desk Supervisor, other Hotel Clerks, Hotel Manager, Housekeeping Staff.
*   **Work:** Hotel Guests, Sales Representatives, Maintenance Staff.
*   **Personal:** Daughter, Mother (for childcare support), Friends.
*   **Specific Challenge (Career):** Potential Mentor (e.g., Hotel Manager), Training Staff.
