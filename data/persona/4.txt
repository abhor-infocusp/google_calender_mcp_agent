**Persona:**

Meet Sarah, a mid-career software salesperson at a growing cybersecurity firm. She's driven, ambitious, and thrives on closing deals. Outside of work, Sarah enjoys hiking and spending time with her family.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Routines:**

*   **Role:** Account Executive
*   **Responsibilities:** Generating leads, conducting product demos, negotiating contracts, and exceeding sales quotas.
*   **Daily/Weekly Routine:** Prospecting calls, client meetings (virtual and in-person), updating CRM, preparing proposals, and attending sales team meetings.
*   **Main Challenges:** Overcoming client objections, competition from larger companies, keeping up with the rapidly evolving cybersecurity landscape.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Sales Manager (David), Sales Engineer (Mark), Marketing Team (Lead: Emily)
*   **Work:** Potential Clients (various IT Managers/CISOs), Existing Clients (Account Managers), Product Development Team
*   **Personal:** Spouse (Chris), Children (2), Friends from hiking group
*   **Specific Challenge:** Mentor (experienced sales professional from outside the company)
