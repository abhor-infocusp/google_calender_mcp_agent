##################

**Persona:**

Marco is a 35-year-old Cruise Ship Chef from Naples, Italy. He's been working on cruise ships for 10 years, saving money to open his own restaurant back home. He is passionate about authentic Italian cuisine and providing passengers with a memorable dining experience.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Routine:**

*   **Role:** Supervising the preparation and presentation of Italian dishes in one of the ship's main dining rooms.
*   **Responsibilities:** Menu planning, ordering ingredients, managing a team of cooks, ensuring food quality and hygiene standards are met.
*   **Routine:** Long hours, typically 12-14 hours a day. Overseeing breakfast, lunch, and dinner services, managing inventory, training staff, and creating daily specials. A typical week includes a couple of days working from 6 AM to 11 PM and a few days where he can finish earlier.
*   **Challenges:** Limited access to fresh ingredients, maintaining consistency across large quantities, dealing with diverse dietary needs, and being away from family for extended periods.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Sous Chefs, Line Cooks, Dishwashers, Kitchen Staff
*   **Work:** Executive Chef, Food and Beverage Manager, Restaurant Manager, Suppliers
*   **Personal:** Wife and children in Naples, Italy, fellow Italian chefs on other cruise ships
*   **Specific Challenge:** Ship doctor (for food safety concerns), specialized suppliers of specific diet ingredients.
