**Persona:**

Maria Rodriguez is a 35-year-old court clerk working at a busy district court. A single mother, she balances her demanding job with raising her 8-year-old son, often feeling the strain of long hours and emotional cases.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Routines:**

As a court clerk, Maria manages court records, prepares dockets, and assists the judge during proceedings. Her typical day involves organizing case files, swearing in witnesses, and maintaining order in the courtroom. Weekly, she compiles statistical reports and attends training sessions to stay updated on legal procedures. A significant challenge she faces is dealing with the emotional weight of sensitive cases and the pressure of ensuring accuracy in a fast-paced environment.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Judge Thompson, fellow court clerks, court reporters
*   **Work:** Lawyers, police officers, members of the public
*   **Personal:** Her son, her mother (who helps with childcare)
*   **Specific Challenge:** Social worker (for support with difficult cases)
