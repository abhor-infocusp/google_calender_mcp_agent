**Persona:**

Maria Rodriguez is a seasoned family lawyer in a bustling metropolitan area. A compassionate yet firm advocate, Maria has dedicated her career to guiding individuals through the complexities of divorce, child custody battles, and adoption proceedings. A mother herself, she understands the emotional toll these cases take and strives to provide empathetic support alongside legal expertise.

**Role:** Family Lawyer

**Responsibilities:**

*   Counseling clients on legal rights and options in family law matters.
*   Drafting and filing legal documents (petitions, motions, agreements).
*   Negotiating settlements.
*   Representing clients in court hearings and trials.
*   Conducting legal research.

**Typical Routine:**

*   **Daily:** Client consultations, court appearances, document preparation, responding to emails and phone calls.
*   **Weekly:** Negotiating with opposing counsel, attending mediation sessions, legal research.

**Main Challenges:**

*   Managing highly emotional and stressful situations.
*   Balancing zealous advocacy with the best interests of children.
*   Dealing with uncooperative or difficult opposing parties.
*   Maintaining professional boundaries.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Paralegals, legal assistants, other attorneys in the firm.
*   **Work:** Judges, court staff, opposing counsel, social workers, therapists.
*   **Personal:** Spouse/Partner, children, close friends, therapist.
*   **Specific Challenge (Burnout):** Mentor, support group of other family lawyers.
