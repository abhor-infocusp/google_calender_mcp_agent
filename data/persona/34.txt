##################

**Persona:**

Meet Frank, a 52-year-old industrial electrician with 25 years under his belt. He's a no-nonsense guy, dedicated to his job at a large manufacturing plant. Weekends are for fishing and spending time with his grandkids. He takes pride in keeping the plant running smoothly.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Challenges:**

*   **Role:** Industrial Electrician
*   **Responsibilities:** Installing, maintaining, and repairing electrical systems in a manufacturing plant. This includes machinery, control panels, and power distribution systems. He also troubleshoots electrical issues, performs preventative maintenance, and ensures compliance with safety regulations.
*   **Daily/Weekly Routine:** Starts with safety checks, followed by assigned maintenance tasks or responding to breakdowns. Spends time reading schematics, testing circuits, and replacing faulty components. A lot of paperwork involved too.
*   **Main Challenges:** Aging infrastructure, tight deadlines to minimize downtime, and keeping up with ever-changing technology.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other electricians, maintenance technicians, plant engineers.
*   **Work:** Plant Manager, machine operators, safety inspector, suppliers of electrical components.
*   **Personal:** Wife, kids, grandkids, fishing buddies.
*   **Specific Challenge (Aging Infrastructure):** Electrical engineer specializing in upgrades, contractors for large-scale replacements.
