**Persona:**

Maria is a 35-year-old, energetic restaurant manager at a popular Italian bistro in a bustling city neighborhood. She's passionate about food and providing excellent customer service. A self-described "people person," she thrives in the fast-paced environment, though she often finds herself juggling multiple responsibilities. She has a young daughter and tries to maintain a healthy work-life balance, but the unpredictable nature of the restaurant industry makes it tough.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Routine:**

*   **Role:** Oversee all aspects of restaurant operations to ensure efficiency, profitability, and customer satisfaction.
*   **Responsibilities:** Managing staff, inventory, ordering supplies, handling customer complaints, ensuring food quality, and maintaining cleanliness.
*   **Daily/Weekly Routine:** Opening/closing the restaurant, scheduling staff, conducting inventory checks, addressing customer issues, reviewing financial reports, and planning promotions.
*   **Main Challenges:** Staff shortages, rising food costs, managing customer expectations, and dealing with unexpected equipment malfunctions.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Servers, cooks, bartenders, dishwashers, hosts/hostesses.
*   **Work:** Head Chef, restaurant owner, food suppliers, health inspectors, repair technicians.
*   **Personal:** Husband, daughter, babysitter, friends.
*   **Specific Challenges:** Accountant (for financial issues), HR consultant (for staff management).
