**Selected Instance: Service Plumber**

##################

**Persona:**

Meet Maria, a 45-year-old service plumber. She's been working in the field for over 20 years. A single mom, she values the stability and decent income her job provides. She is known in her community for her quick thinking and ability to solve almost any plumbing issue.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Challenges:**

*   **Role:** Service Plumber
*   **Responsibilities:** Responding to service calls, diagnosing plumbing problems (leaks, clogs, fixture issues), performing repairs and replacements, and providing excellent customer service.
*   **Daily Routine:** Receives dispatch calls, drives to various locations, assesses plumbing problems, explains repair options to clients, performs necessary work, and completes paperwork.
*   **Main Challenges:** Dealing with unpredictable schedules, handling difficult customers, diagnosing complex or hidden plumbing issues, and staying up-to-date with new technologies.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Dispatcher, other plumbers at the company.
*   **Work:** Customers, suppliers at the plumbing supply store, building inspectors.
*   **Personal:** Her teenage son, her elderly mother, neighbors.
*   **Specific Challenge:** A mentor who specializes in new plumbing technologies, for when she needs advice on newer systems.
