Dr. Emily Carter is a dedicated pediatrician working in a bustling suburban clinic. She balances her demanding career with raising two young children.

**Role:** Pediatrician

**Responsibilities:** Conducting routine check-ups, diagnosing and treating illnesses in children, administering vaccinations, advising parents on child health and development, and managing chronic conditions.

**Daily/Weekly Routine:** Emily typically sees 20-25 patients daily, ranging from newborns to adolescents. She spends time charting, consulting with specialists, and attending weekly staff meetings.

**Main Challenges:** Communicating effectively with children and anxious parents, keeping up with the latest medical advancements, and managing her work-life balance.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Nurses (Sarah, Maria), Medical Assistant (Tom), Receptionist (Karen), other Pediatricians (Dr. Lee, Dr. Patel).
*   **Work:** Parents, Child Life Specialists, School Nurses, Pharmacists.
*   **Personal:** Spouse (David), Children (Lily, Ben), her own Physician (Dr. Johnson).
*   **Specific Challenge (Burnout):** Therapist (Dr. Ramirez).
