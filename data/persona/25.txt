Let's randomly select **Service Plumber**.

##################

**Persona:**

Maria is a 45-year-old service plumber, a single mother of two teenagers. She's been working in the trade for over 20 years and prides herself on her problem-solving skills and ability to put customers at ease.

**Role, Responsibilities, and Challenges:**

*   **Role:** Service Plumber
*   **Responsibilities:** Responding to service calls for residential and commercial clients, diagnosing plumbing issues (leaks, clogs, malfunctioning fixtures), performing repairs and replacements, providing cost estimates, and ensuring customer satisfaction.
*   **Daily/Weekly Routine:** Starts the day by reviewing the schedule and prioritizing calls based on urgency. Drives to different locations, assesses plumbing problems, performs repairs, and completes paperwork. Handles emergency calls after hours.
*   **Main Challenges:** Dealing with difficult customers, unexpected complications in old plumbing systems, keeping up with new technologies and regulations, and balancing work with family responsibilities.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Dispatcher, other service plumbers, plumbing apprentices.
*   **Work:** Customers, suppliers, building managers, inspectors.
*   **Personal:** Her children, her mother, friends from her plumbing trade school.
*   **Specific Challenge:** A therapist to deal with the stress of single parenting and work.
