**Persona:**

Meet Jake, a 45-year-old industrial electrician at a large manufacturing plant. He's a practical, no-nonsense guy with a good sense of humor, who takes pride in keeping the machines running smoothly. Weekends are spent fishing or tinkering with his classic car.

**Role, Responsibilities, Routines, and Challenges:**

*   **Role:** Industrial Electrician
*   **Responsibilities:** Maintaining and repairing electrical systems and equipment in a manufacturing plant. This includes motors, transformers, control panels, and PLCs.
*   **Daily/Weekly Routines:** Responding to equipment breakdowns, performing preventative maintenance, inspecting electrical systems, installing new equipment, and troubleshooting complex electrical issues. Weekly tasks involve safety audits and ordering supplies.
*   **Main Challenges:** Diagnosing problems quickly to minimize downtime, working in hazardous environments, keeping up with new technologies, and ensuring all work meets strict safety regulations.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other electricians, maintenance mechanics, plant engineers
*   **Work:** Plant manager, machine operators, suppliers of electrical equipment
*   **Personal:** Wife, kids, fishing buddies
*   **Specific Challenge:** Electrical safety inspector
