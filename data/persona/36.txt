**Persona:** Maria is a 52-year-old school bus driver in a suburban town. A mother of two grown children, she enjoys the routine and the kids on her route. She finds purpose in ensuring their safe transport to and from school daily.

**Role:** School Bus Driver

**Responsibilities:**

*   Safe operation of a school bus.
*   Following a set route and schedule.
*   Maintaining order and discipline on the bus.
*   Conducting pre-trip and post-trip inspections.
*   Communicating with school staff and parents.

**Typical Week:**

*   Morning and afternoon routes, five days a week.
*   Vehicle safety checks before each run.
*   Occasional field trips or special event transportation.
*   Dealing with student behavior issues.

**Main Challenges:**

*   Maintaining student discipline.
*   Navigating traffic and adverse weather conditions.
*   Adhering to strict schedules.
*   Dealing with difficult parents or school staff.
*   Vehicle maintenance issues.

**Relevant People:**

*   **Team:** Other bus drivers, mechanics, dispatchers.
*   **Work:** School principal, teachers, school secretaries, students.
*   **Personal:** Husband, children, neighbors.
